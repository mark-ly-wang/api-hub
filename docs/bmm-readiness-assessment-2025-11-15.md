**Impact:** May affect Chinese user adoption

**Mitigation:**
- Architecture已预留支付抽象层 (PaymentProvider interface)
- Phase 2可无缝替换为微信/支付宝
- MVP阶段目标用户为技术创作者,Stripe接受度较高

**Recommendation:**
✅ **ACCEPT RISK** for MVP
